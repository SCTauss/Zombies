# Role: Medic (owner: Lane B)

> Draft. Items marked *(idea)* are suggestions. Big changes go through `DECISIONS.md`.

**Feels like:** Quarantine Zone (Steam) × Animal Hospital (Roblox).
**Fantasy:** The gate and the ward are yours. You decide who comes in, who
gets treated, and what the virus really is.

## Core loops

### 1. Check-in
Survivors arrive at the gate. Inspect their documents and symptoms, then
admit, quarantine or turn them away.
- *(idea)* Inspection tools: ID papers, a temperature check, a bite search,
  questions. Symptoms follow this run's virus, so early runs are guesswork
  until research makes them clearer.
- *(idea)* Forged documents connect to the Politician's paperwork.
- *(idea)* Admit an infected person and the outbreak starts inside the walls.
  Reject too many and the camp dies of a labor shortage.

### 2. Hospital
Heal and treat the camp: wounded soldiers, sick citizens, the infected.
- *(idea)* Bed management, triage priority, diagnosis mini-games, treatments
  that use medicine bought with the budget.
- *(idea)* Hospital capacity and equipment are built by Labor.

### 3. Research
Study the virus, the zombies and treatments.
- *(idea)* The virus is randomized each run (transmission, incubation,
  symptoms, weaknesses). Research reveals the rules and unlocks treatments,
  tests, vaccines, and zombie weaknesses the Military can use.
- *(idea)* Samples come from Military expeditions and from patients.
- *(open)* Is a cure a way to win a run?

## Gives to other roles
Population (admitted survivors), healed soldiers and workers, infection
reports, research unlocks.

## Needs from other roles
Samples (Military), beds and labs (Labor), medicine budget and quarantine
policy (Politician).

## When no one plays this role *(open)*
*(idea)* Admit everyone after a basic check, heal slowly, and do no research.

## Open questions
- Can the Medic see infection levels in the camp directly, or only through tests?
- Is there a morally grey "treatment" option (euthanasia, experiments)? It
  would fit the tone, but the owner decides.

## Suggested prototype order
Check-in desk with generated survivors (standalone) → admit/reject affects mock
camp state → hospital beds → virus model and research tree.
