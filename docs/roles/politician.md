# Role: Politician (owner: Lane B)

> Draft. Items marked *(idea)* are suggestions. Big changes go through `DECISIONS.md`.

**Feels like:** Pathologic 2 × political/governance games (think Papers, Please
desk work, Suzerain-style choices, Democracy-style policy sliders).
**Fantasy:** You hold the pen. Every signature saves someone and costs someone else.
Rise to the top, or lose the camp to your rivals.

## Core loops

### 1. Signing documents (daily, repeating)
A stack of paperwork arrives every day: requests, permits, requisitions,
reports, petitions. Read, sign, reject, or put aside.
- *(idea)* Documents come from the other roles' activity, for example
  Military requisitions, Medic quarantine orders and Labor building permits.
  The paperwork reflects what your friends are doing.
- *(idea)* Some documents are traps: forged, bribed, or written by an opponent.
- *(idea)* Unsigned documents pile up and slow down other roles.

### 2. Budget
Divide the camp's money and resources between roles, maintenance, wages and
projects. Other players' budgets come from you.
- *(idea)* Tax and ration levels change income and approval.
- *(idea)* Debt and loans from outside factions.

### 3. Policy
Set policies that affect infection, economy, approval, debuffs, quarantine and
more. Most policies are trade-offs.
- Examples *(ideas)*: curfew (safer nights, lower morale), mandatory testing
  (slows check-in, catches the infected), martial law (Military buff, approval
  crash), rationing, conscription, open borders, executing the bitten.
- *(idea)* Policies take days to take effect, and you can see them coming.

### 4. Roster
The politicians and leaders who back you: your party. Recruit, train and
improve them, and give them posts.
- *(idea)* Each member has a passive bonus and a loyalty meter. A disloyal
  member defects to the opposition.
- *(idea)* Opponents and rival parties have rosters too, and elections or
  no-confidence votes happen *(open: is there an election system?)*.

### 5. Judge
You are called to judge citizens and NPCs accused of wrongdoing. Every verdict
is a trade-off.
- You can get your friends (even other players) out of trouble, but it costs
  reputation and can cause a rebellion.
- *(idea)* Evidence comes from the other roles: Medic records, Military
  reports, Labor witnesses.

## Gives to other roles
Budget, policies, verdicts, permits. Overall camp stability (approval).

## Needs from other roles
Reports (infection, defense, production), evidence, and citizens who don't riot.

## When no one plays this role *(open)*
*(idea)* An automatic council follows a fixed policy set, signs everything,
and splits the budget evenly.

## Open questions
- Is there a way to lose specific to the Politician, like being overthrown? What happens to that player then?
- Are elections and rival parties real systems, or just flavor?
- How much real power over other *players* (not NPCs) should the Politician have?

## Suggested prototype order
Document signing (standalone, mock camp state) → budget screen → 3–5 policies →
judge case → roster.
